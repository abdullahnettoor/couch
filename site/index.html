The Remote

Your phone is the remote.

Scan the QR code on the Screen once. After that your phone is a D-pad, volume and Now Playing, and every press moves the highlight on the TV. Pair as many phones as you like; guests can join in.

  • No app to install on the phone. The Remote is a web page.
  • Mac keyboard and trackpad work too, for when you are at the Mac.
  • The Screen and every Remote always agree on where you are.

Apps

The Apps you already use.

YouTube opens as its own TV experience, and Netflix opens on Netflix's own website. You sign in on the Screen with your own accounts. Couch gives no access to anything you could not open in a browser.

  • YouTube looks and behaves like the YouTube app on a smart TV.
  • Netflix is driven from the Remote with a clear focus ring.
  • More Apps can join the list later.

The Library

A folder of videos becomes a shelf.

Point Couch at the folders where your videos live. They appear as a tidy shelf with a frame from each video, a cleaned-up title and where you stopped. Press play and it resumes from there.

  • Plays through its own built-in player, with subtitles and volume from the Remote.
  • Continue watching sits on Home, one press away.
  • Film and show details are optional, and the Library works fully without them.

Calm by design

Nothing leaves your Mac but what you open.

  • No Couch accountThere is nothing to sign up for, and no profiles. The household shares one Screen.
  • No cloudYour Library, your place in each video and your settings stay on the Mac.
  • No trackingNo analytics in the app and none on this site. No cookies.
  • Open sourceRead every line, or build it yourself, under GPL-3.0-or-later.

Couch talks to the services you open (YouTube, Netflix) and, if you allow it, to TMDB for film details. Nothing else.

Setup

Ready in two minutes.

  1. 1

    Install

    Download Couch, drag it to Applications, and open it on the Mac that is plugged into your TV.

  2. 2

    Scan the QR code

    In Settings choose Add a phone. Scan the code with your phone's camera, once.

  3. 3

    Open an App

    Press OK on YouTube, Netflix or the Library. Sit back.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it free?

Yes. Couch is free and open source. On Gumroad you choose what to pay, including nothing, and you can also download it from GitHub Releases at no cost.

Do I need accounts?

Not for Couch: there is no Couch account and no profiles. YouTube and Netflix use your own accounts, which you sign in to on the Screen.

Does it work with Netflix?

Couch shows Netflix's own website inside the Screen and lets you drive it from the Remote. You need your own Netflix account, and playback follows what Netflix's website allows on a Mac.

Why does macOS warn me?

Couch is an early release and Apple has not notarised it yet, so macOS checks with you before the first open. Right-click Couch in Applications, choose Open, then Open. You only do this once.

Is it open source?

Yes, under GPL-3.0-or-later. The code is on GitHub.

What about Windows or Linux?

Not for now. Couch is built for a Mac on Apple silicon running macOS 14 or later. Any phone with a browser can be the Remote; it is tested on Android.
